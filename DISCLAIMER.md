Buffett, Elon Musk, Andrej Karpathy, Andrew Chen, Nir Eyal, Kenya Hara, and others) are
**educational interpretations** of thinking frameworks documented in public sources — books,

  **not** professional, legal, medical, financial, or investment advice. In particular,

  security.
